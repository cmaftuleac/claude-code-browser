This folder is for files with extensions the app doesn't recognize.

When you open `mystery.xyz` (regenerated by samples/scripts/gen.mjs) the app
should default to the Info view, since `.xyz` isn't in any of the known sets.
The Info pane shows name, size, full path, mtime, and a binary badge.

You can also drop your own oddball files here — `.dat`, `.bin`, weird
proprietary formats, anything — to confirm the unknown fallback is working.
